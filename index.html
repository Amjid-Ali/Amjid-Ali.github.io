Amjid Ali

Computer Vision Engineer building real-time video analytics systems that run in production on live industrial CCTV. At VoltaON I develop safety-detection AI deployed at POSCO's Gwangyang steel plant and for two other Korean manufacturers — fire and smoke detection, worker safety monitoring, and heavy-equipment collision detection, running continuously on plant cameras. My work spans the full delivery path: building datasets, training detection models, optimizing inference, and shipping them as GPU video pipelines with DeepStream, YOLO, OpenCV, FFmpeg and MediaMTX, served through FastAPI. What I am best at is the part that is not the model — making detection hold up on real footage with smoke, glare, night operation and heavy occlusion, across many simultaneous camera streams. I hold an M.S. in Software from Sejong University, where I researched audio-visual anomaly recognition.

    Research Assistant & Teaching Assistant

    June 2020 ~ March 2024:     Digital Image Processing Lab, Department of Computer Science, Islamia College Peshawar, KP, Pakistan

    My first computer vision research role, where I moved from classical image processing into deep learning.

    Early Fall Prediction in the Elderly Using Gait Analysis — funded by the European Union through its Research and Innovation Programme. Falls are a leading cause of injury among older adults, and the goal was to identify people at risk before an incident rather than detect one after it happens. I applied machine learning to spatiotemporal motion data to model gait irregularities — stride timing, balance and movement patterns — and turn them into a usable risk signal. I also built the interface clinicians and researchers used to review results, and contributed to the project reporting for this international smart healthcare collaboration on aging populations.

    Edge face recognition — I developed a real-time face recognition system deployed on a Raspberry Pi, covering camera integration and live inference under tight compute and memory constraints. It was my first experience of making a vision model hold up on constrained hardware rather than in a notebook, which is directly what my work on real-time video pipelines depends on today.

    Wider lab work — I supported research across image classification, object detection and segmentation, and worked on my undergraduate thesis, Visually Assisted System using Blockchain, combining deep learning video summarisation with Ethereum smart contracts for tamper-proof storage and verification. As a Teaching Assistant I ran lab sessions and supervised junior students through their coursework and final year projects.
